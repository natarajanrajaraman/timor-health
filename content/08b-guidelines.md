# ⚠️ The national documents exist, and almost nobody can find them

**Timor-Leste's medicines regulator publishes a substantial document library on a live government
server — and its own website menu links to none of it.**

The National Regulatory Authority portal at `apps.ms.gov.tl/dnfm/` has menu sections for exactly what
you would want: *Medikamentu Nasionál*, *Tratamentu Padraun* (standard treatment), *Ai-moruk Esensiál*
(essential medicines), decree-laws, ministerial diplomas and circulars. **Verified 2026-08-25: every
one of those sections renders an empty page** — a heading and nothing else.

Meanwhile **[`apps.ms.gov.tl/dnfm/docs/`](https://apps.ms.gov.tl/dnfm/docs/) is an open, browsable
directory** holding **40+ files** across `doc/`, `guidelines/`, `law/`, `policy/` and `seminar/`. Every
link below was fetched and confirmed to return a real PDF on **2026-08-25**.

**This is the single most useful thing in this document for anyone who needs the actual national
paperwork.** It is reachable by anyone, indexed by nobody.

edition reported that the published national clinical guidelines were largely from 2004 and 2010.

# Disease programme guidelines and strategies

These are **not** in the DNFM library — they sit with WHO and ReliefWeb.

**Tuberculosis**
- **Comprehensive TB Guidelines for the National Tuberculosis Programme, V Edition** — covers
  drug-susceptible TB, drug-resistant TB, patient care and latent TB infection.
  [Via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/timor-leste/democratic-republic-timor-leste-ministry-health-comprehensive-tb-guidelines-national-tuberculosis-program-v-edition)
- **National Strategic Plan for Ending TB, 2020–2024** —
  [via ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/report/timor-leste/national-strategic-plan-ending-tb-2020-2024).
  ⚠️ **Expired. No published successor was located** — the same pattern as the HRH plan (§6).

**HIV, STIs and viral hepatitis**
- **Guidelines for Antiretroviral Therapy and Management of Opportunistic Infections for Children,
  Adolescents and Adults Living with HIV** —
  [WHO Timor-Leste](https://www.who.int/timorleste/publications/guidelines-for-antiretroviral-therapy--management-of-opportunistic-infections-for-children--adolescents---adults-living-with-hiv)
- **[National Strategic Plan for HIV, STIs and Viral Hepatitis 2022–2026](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/timor-leste-docs/8_timor-leste-hhs-nsp_08-may-2023.pdf)** —
  an integrated plan across all three, developed with WHO technical assistance. ⚠️ **Ends 2026.**
- [Joint HIV/TB External Review Mission 2023](https://www.who.int/timorleste/publications/the-joint-hiv-tb-external--review-mission-2023)

**Non-communicable diseases**
- The Ministry has **adopted and implemented WHO's Package of Essential Noncommunicable Disease
  interventions (PEN)** nationally — and Timor-Leste-specific PEN documents exist. Two were opened
  and verified 2026-08-28 in the partner document library below: ***Package of Essential
  Noncommunicable Diseases Interventions in Timor-Leste*** (Ministry of Health with the WHO country
  office, **2017**, 74 pp) and ***Protocols for organizing, implementation and monitoring of
  people-centred PEN interventions in Primary Health Care, Version 1*** (94 pp; file dated December
  2021), with a Tetum implementation guide alongside. *(The previous edition flagged the existence
  of a TL-specific PEN protocol as unverified; that flag is now resolved.)*
- The substantive evidence base is the **[National NCD and facility-readiness survey 2023](https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/386866)** (§3).

**Nutrition**
- **[National Health Sector Nutrition Strategic Plan 2022–2026](https://www.unicef.org/timorleste/media/4766/file/unicef%20National%20Health%20Sector%20Nutrition%20Strategic%20Plan%202022-2026%20Eng-low%20res.pdf)** ⚠️ **Ends 2026.**

⚠️ **Notice how many national plans expire in 2026** — TB (2024, already lapsed), HIV/STI/hepatitis,
nutrition, and both Global Fund grants (§7). **A partner planning beyond 2026 should ask what replaces
them**, not assume continuity.

# The partner document library

With the owner's permission, this document links a partner-maintained Google Drive collection:
**[Health Timor-SG Resources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1CWgBpaObmQYRaHnDayizum_ugJdvb-Ft)**,
maintained alongside the TL-SG Health Collective site (see the header). Anyone with the link can
view. Inventoried **2026-08-28**: roughly a hundred files across eight topic folders — maternal
health; child health, school health and nutrition; infectious diseases; non-communicable diseases;
gender-based violence; health system and primary-care guidelines; Ministry partnership manuals; and
in-service training materials.

**What it holds that the government's own library does not** (titles as filed; items marked ✓ were
opened and verified 2026-08-28):

- ✓ The **2022 primary-care STG** and the **December 2021 national ANC and PNC protocols** (see the
  clinical-guidelines section above)
- ✓ The **Timor-Leste PEN documents** — the 2017 package, the Version-1 protocols and a Tetum
  implementation guide (see NCDs above)
- The **Ministry of Health Partnership Manual (2022)** in English, Portuguese and Tetum, with its
  annexes — the Ministry's own manual for how partners engage with the health system
- The **National Health Sector Monitoring and Evaluation Guidelines 2024–2030** with both
  supplementary books, including the M&E framework for primary health care
- ✓ ***Gender-Based Violence and Healthcare in Timor-Leste*** (Wild, Langford, de Araújo et al., La
  Trobe University, first published 2023; English and Tetum editions, 196 pp) — an open-licence
  clinical textbook, filed alongside the **GBV National Action Plan 2022–2032** and the national GBV
  facilitator curriculum
- TB programme documents: the ministerially approved **NTP Manual (2014, Tetum and English)**,
  **childhood TB guidelines (2016)** and **extrapulmonary TB guidelines** — complementing the
  WHO-hosted V Edition comprehensive guidelines above
- **EMTCT guidelines for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B (2019)**, draft national STI guidelines, and
  the 2022 antimicrobial guidelines
- The **IMCI Timor-Leste adaptation** with its technical updates; **SNIP immunisation guidelines**
  (Tetum); the **food-based dietary guidelines** (English and Tetum); the **TL Food and Nutrition
  Survey 2020**
- The **Essential Service Package for PHC**, the Quality Strategic Plan 2020–2024 executive summary,
  the HRH strategy, the **Disability Action Plan 2023–2030**, the National Eye Plan and a palliative
  care guideline
- Maluk Timor's **in-service training and curriculum-development materials** for the integrated
  primary-care programme
- Topic folders of published research on maternal and child health, nutrition, NCDs and infectious
  diseases in Timor-Leste
- ✓ **Guterres Correia's historical overview of the Timorese health system (2025)** — the source used
  for the historical financing narrative in §5, added to the library by this project on 2026-08-28

⚠️ **The library is a living folder, not an archive** — contents can change after this inventory
date, and inclusion is a filing decision by its maintainers, not a Ministry endorsement.

# ⚠️ How to check this yourself

**Do not rely on this list staying accurate.** Open
**[`apps.ms.gov.tl/dnfm/docs/`](https://apps.ms.gov.tl/dnfm/docs/)** and browse — it is a plain
directory index with modification dates, so you can see what has been added since this edition. The
[partner library](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1CWgBpaObmQYRaHnDayizum_ugJdvb-Ft) can be
browsed the same way.

**And tell us what you find**, especially any nationally issued guideline not yet listed here. The
2022 STG surfaced exactly this way — through a partner's collection, not a government website. That
is the most valuable correction anyone could send this document.
